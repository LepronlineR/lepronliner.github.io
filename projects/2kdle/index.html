2KDLE

2KDLE is an NBA 2K player rating guessing game. You pick a player, and then the player's ratings will be shown (team, conference, position, overall rating, and different skills) as a comparison to the hidden player. You will have to keep guessing (7 guesses) to get the hidden player. The game includes a leaderboard for all three difficulties in the game, if you sign in you can keep track of your streak on the leaderboard. The game utilizes the NBA2K API and may not be completely updated, the images are taken from https://www.2kratings.com/.

Green means the exact value/answer, yellow means that the rating is within 3 points, and gray means that the rating is 3 or more away. The arrows display if that rating is below or above that number.
